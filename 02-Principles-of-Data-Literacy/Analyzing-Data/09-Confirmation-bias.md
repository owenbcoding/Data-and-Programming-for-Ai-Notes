# Confirmation Bias

## Understanding Confirmation Bias
Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, favor, and recall information in a way that confirms one's preexisting beliefs or hypotheses, while giving disproportionately less consideration to alternative possibilities.

## How Confirmation Bias Manifests

### Data Selection
- **Selective data**: Choosing data that supports beliefs
- **Ignoring contradictory**: Ignoring contradictory evidence
- **Cherry-picking**: Selecting favorable examples
- **Data mining**: Searching for confirming patterns
- **Sample bias**: Biased sample selection

### Data Interpretation
- **Biased interpretation**: Interpreting ambiguous evidence as confirming
- **Rationalization**: Explaining away contradictory evidence
- **Selective recall**: Remembering confirming evidence
- **Ambiguity**: Interpreting ambiguity as support
- **Pattern recognition**: Seeing patterns that confirm beliefs

### Analysis Method Selection
- **Method choice**: Choosing methods that confirm beliefs
- **Parameter tuning**: Tuning parameters to confirm
- **Model selection**: Selecting models that confirm
- **Assumption choices**: Making confirming assumptions
- **Testing**: Testing only confirming hypotheses

## Psychological Mechanisms

### Cognitive Dissonance
- **Definition**: Mental discomfort from conflicting beliefs
- **Effect**: Reduces dissonance by confirming beliefs
- **Impact**: Biased information processing
- **Example**: Disregarding contradictory data
- **Solution**: Awareness and deliberate consideration

### Heuristic Processing
- **Definition**: Mental shortcuts for decision making
- **Effect**: Quick but potentially biased processing
- **Impact**: Confirms existing beliefs
- **Example**: Relying on familiar patterns
- **Solution**: Analytical thinking

### Motivated Reasoning
- **Definition**: Reasoning motivated by desired conclusions
- **Effect**: Biased toward desired conclusions
- **Impact**: Confirms preferred beliefs
- **Example**: Interpreting data to support desired outcome
- **Solution**: Objective analysis
