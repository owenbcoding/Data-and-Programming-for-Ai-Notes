# Statistical Thinking Lab: Movie Statistics

## Project Overview
This lab applies statistical thinking concepts to analyze movie data, practicing the skills learned in the Principles of Data Literacy course.

## Project Description
Analyze a dataset of movies to explore statistical concepts including central tendency, variability, distributions, and relationships between variables.

## Dataset Variables
- **Title**: Movie title
- **Genre**: Movie genre (Action, Comedy, Drama, etc.)
- **Year**: Release year
- **Rating**: IMDb rating (1-10)
- **Runtime**: Movie runtime in minutes
- **Budget**: Production budget in millions
- **Gross**: Box office gross in millions
- **Director**: Director name
- **Actor**: Main actor/actress

## Statistical Concepts Applied

### Central Tendency
- **Mean**: Average movie rating, runtime, budget
- **Median**: Median gross revenue, runtime
- **Mode**: Most common genre, director
- **Comparison**: Compare mean vs. median for skewed variables

### Variability
- **Range**: Range of ratings, budgets, gross revenue
- **Standard deviation**: Variability in ratings, runtime
- **IQR**: Interquartile range for budgets, gross
- **Coefficient of variation**: Relative variability

### Distributions
- **Rating distribution**: Shape of rating distribution
- **Budget distribution**: Typically right-skewed
- **Gross distribution**: Typically right-skewed
- **Genre distribution**: Frequency of genres
- **Normality assessment**: Are ratings normally distributed?

### Relationships
- **Rating vs. gross**: Does higher rating correlate with higher gross?
- **Budget vs. gross**: Relationship between budget and revenue
- **Runtime vs. rating**: Does movie length affect ratings?
- **Genre vs. rating**: Do different genres have different ratings?
- **Year trends**: Changes over time

## Analysis Steps

### Step 1: Data Exploration
- Load and examine the dataset
- Check data quality and missing values
- Calculate basic summary statistics
- Examine distributions of key variables
- Identify any outliers or unusual patterns

### Step 2: Descriptive Statistics
- Calculate mean, median, mode for numerical variables
- Calculate variability measures (range, SD, IQR)
- Examine distributions using histograms
- Compare central tendency measures
- Assess skewness of distributions

### Step 3: Group Analysis
- Analyze statistics by genre
- Compare ratings across genres
- Compare budgets across genres
- Compare gross revenue across genres
- Identify which genres are most profitable

### Step 4: Relationship Analysis
- Calculate correlations between numerical variables
- Create scatter plots for key relationships
- Analyze rating vs. gross relationship
- Analyze budget vs. gross relationship
- Consider confounding variables

### Step 5: Time Analysis
- Analyze trends over time
- Changes in average ratings over years
- Changes in budgets over years
- Changes in gross revenue over years
- Identify significant trends

### Step 6: Advanced Analysis
- Identify and investigate outliers
- Analyze relationships controlling for other variables
- Consider interaction effects
- Apply statistical thinking to draw conclusions
- Communicate findings effectively

## Expected Findings

### Distribution Patterns
- **Ratings**: Likely approximately normal distribution
- **Budgets**: Right-skewed distribution (few very expensive movies)
- **Gross**: Right-skewed distribution (few blockbusters)
- **Runtime**: Slightly right-skewed distribution
- **Genres**: Some genres more common than others

### Relationship Patterns
- **Rating-gross**: Positive correlation (higher rated movies tend to earn more)
- **Budget-gross**: Positive correlation (higher budget often leads to higher gross)
- **Runtime-rating**: Weak or no relationship
- **Genre differences**: Some genres tend to have higher ratings or gross

### Temporal Patterns
- **Budget inflation**: Budgets tend to increase over time
- **Rating stability**: Average ratings relatively stable over time
- **Gross trends**: Gross revenue trends over time
- **Genre popularity**: Changes in genre popularity over time
