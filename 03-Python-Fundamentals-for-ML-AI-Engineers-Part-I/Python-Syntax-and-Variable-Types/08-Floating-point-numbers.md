# Floating-Point Numbers

Floating-point numbers are numbers with decimal points. In Python, they are represented by the `float` type.

## Characteristics
- Numbers with decimal points
- Can be positive, negative, or zero
- Used for fractional quantities
- Limited precision due to floating-point representation
- Scientific notation supported

## Uses
- Scientific calculations
- Financial calculations
- Measurements
- Probabilities and statistics
- Physical quantities

## Precision Considerations
Floating-point numbers have limited precision due to how computers store them. This can lead to small rounding errors in calculations.

## Float Operations
Floats support all basic arithmetic operations and have similar behavior to integers in most operations.

## Type Conversion
Floats can be converted to integers (losing decimal precision) and strings can be converted to floats when they represent valid numbers.
