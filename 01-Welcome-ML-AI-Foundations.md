## Key vocabulary introduced at the start

- **Machine Learning (ML):** Systems that learn patterns from data rather than following hand-coded rules.
- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The broader field of building systems that perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence.
- **AI Engineer:** Builds and deploys AI/ML systems in production (vs. a researcher who develops new algorithms).
- **Data pipeline:** The chain of steps that moves raw data through cleaning, transformation, and into a model or report.
- **Feature:** An individual measurable variable used as input to a model (e.g. age, BMI, region).
- **Label / target:** The output variable a model is trained to predict.
- **Overfitting:** A model that memorises training data and fails on new data.
- **Underfitting:** A model too simple to capture the real pattern in the data.
